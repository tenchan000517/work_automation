```
Create an anime character reference sheet in the style of Makoto Shinkai films.

The character is a 17-year-old Japanese male high school student with short black hair and gentle but determined dark brown eyes. He has a slim build typical of a teenager.

He is wearing a traditional Japanese school uniform (gakuran) in navy blue color with gold buttons.

Show the character from multiple angles: front view, side profile, and three-quarter view. Include both full body poses and upper body close-ups.

Use a clean white background. The art style should have detailed shading, soft lighting, and the atmospheric quality seen in films like "Your Name" or "Weathering with You".
```

```
Create an anime character reference sheet in the style of Makoto Shinkai films.

The character is the same person, now a 28-year-old Japanese male factory worker. He has the same short black hair but with a more mature, confident expression. His eyes show focus and determination developed through work experience.

He is wearing a dark blue industrial work coverall uniform. No helmet or hard hat.

Show the character from multiple angles: front view, side profile, and three-quarter view. Include both full body poses and upper body close-ups.

Use a clean white background. The art style should maintain consistency with the high school version while showing his growth and maturity.
```

### 表情シート

```
Create an anime facial expression reference sheet in the style of Makoto Shinkai films.

The character is a young Japanese male with short black hair and dark brown eyes.

Show six different expressions in close-up face shots:
1. Neutral, calm expression

3. Determined, resolute look
4. Intense focus while working
5. Wiping sweat with a tired but satisfied expression

Arrange the expressions in a 2x3 grid on a clean white background. Maintain consistent facial features across all expressions.

## シーン1：日常と迷い（0-5秒）

**フェーズ:** 導入（Past）
**主人公:** 17歳（学生服）
**BGM:** 静かなピアノソロ
**ナレーション:** 「あの頃、僕は何かになりたかった。」

### 開始フレーム

```
Create an anime scene in the style of Makoto Shinkai films.

A 17-year-old Japanese male high school student with short black hair stands on a school rooftop at golden hour sunset. He is wearing a navy blue gakuran school uniform. Shot from behind, showing his back as he looks up at the dramatic orange and purple sky.

The clouds are detailed and luminous, catching the warm light. Lens flare from the setting sun creates an emotional, contemplative atmosphere. The school building railings are visible in the foreground.

Cinematic composition, 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

### 終了フレーム

```
Create an anime scene in the style of Makoto Shinkai films.

Same scene as before: a 17-year-old Japanese male high school student with short black hair on a school rooftop at sunset, shot from behind.

The camera has tilted upward, now showing more of the vast sky filled with dramatic clouds in orange, pink, and purple. The student figure is smaller in the frame, emphasizing the expansive sky above him.

The clouds appear to be moving fast, suggesting the passage of time and change. Cinematic composition, 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

### カメラワーク指示（動画生成用）

```
Camera pans slowly upward to the sky. Clouds are moving fast. Wind blowing his hair.
```

## シーン2：目覚め（5-10秒）

**フェーズ:** 導入（Past）
**主人公:** 17歳（学生服）
**BGM:** ピアノ + 環境音
**ナレーション:** （なし）
**演出意図:** 迷いから気づきへ。下向き→上向きで感情の変化を表現

### 開始フレーム

```
Create an anime scene in the style of Makoto Shinkai films.

A 17-year-old Japanese male high school student with short black hair sits alone in a classroom after school. He is wearing a navy blue gakuran school uniform. His head is bowed down, looking at his hands or the desk, lost in thought.

Late afternoon sunlight streams through the windows, creating long shadows. The empty classroom has a quiet, contemplative atmosphere. Dust particles float in the golden light beams.

His posture suggests uncertainty about his future, a young person searching for meaning. Melancholic but beautiful lighting. Cinematic composition, 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

### 終了フレーム

```
Create an anime scene in the style of Makoto Shinkai films.

Same classroom scene. The 17-year-old student with short black hair has suddenly lifted his head up. His eyes are now wide open, catching the warm sunlight streaming through the window.

His expression shows a moment of realization, as if he just discovered something important. Light reflects in his dark brown eyes, giving them a spark of hope and determination. His posture has shifted from defeated to alert and inspired.

The golden afternoon light illuminates his face beautifully. A turning point moment captured. Cinematic composition, 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

### カメラワーク指示（動画生成用）

```
The student sits with head bowed. He slowly raises his head, eyes opening wider as if struck by realization. Warm sunlight catches his face. Expression transforms from uncertainty to determination.
```

## シーン3：現場への一歩（10-15秒）

**フェーズ:** 転換（Change）
**主人公:** 28歳（作業着）
**BGM:** ストリングス + ドラム ビルドアップ
**ナレーション:** 「熱くなれる場所を探していた。」

### 開始フレーム

```
Create an anime scene in the style of Makoto Shinkai films.

Low angle shot focusing on the legs and feet of a 28-year-old male factory worker. He is wearing dark blue work coveralls and industrial safety boots. He is walking towards a massive factory entrance.

The factory doors are partially open, with bright orange light glowing from inside, creating a dramatic contrast with the darker exterior. Dust particles dance in the beams of orange light spilling out.

The atmosphere suggests he is about to enter a new world. Cinematic composition, 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

### 終了フレーム

```
Create an anime scene in the style of Makoto Shinkai films.

Same low angle shot of the factory worker in dark blue coveralls walking forward. He has now stepped closer to the factory entrance.

The massive factory doors have opened wider, and intense orange light floods the entire frame. The worker's silhouette is backlit by the warm industrial glow from inside.

The orange light represents the heat of molten metal and the passion of the work ahead. Cinematic composition, 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

### カメラワーク指示（動画生成用）

```
Walking forward, the factory door opens slowly, orange light floods the screen.
```

## シーン4：注湯作業 - クライマックス（15-20秒）

**フェーズ:** サビ（Action）
**主人公:** 28歳（作業着）
**BGM:** サビ開始（歌詞付き楽曲）
**ナレーション:** （なし、歌詞と映像で魅せる）

### 開始フレーム

```
Create an anime scene in the style of Makoto Shinkai films.

Dramatic industrial foundry scene. A 28-year-old Japanese male worker with short black hair, wearing dark blue coveralls and heat-resistant gloves, controls a large ladle.

Molten iron glows intensely orange, about to be poured. The scene is lit dramatically by the glowing metal. Sparks begin to fly. The worker's face is illuminated by the orange glow, showing intense concentration.

Dark industrial background with dramatic contrast between shadows and the bright molten metal. Cinematic composition, 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

### 終了フレーム

```
Create an anime scene in the style of Makoto Shinkai films.

Same foundry scene. The 28-year-old worker in dark blue coveralls is now actively pouring molten iron from the ladle into a casting mold.

Brilliant orange molten metal streams downward. Hundreds of sparks fly dynamically in all directions, some towards the camera. The scene is alive with energy and heat.

The worker's silhouette is dramatic against the intense orange glow. Steam and sparks fill the air. This is the climactic moment of creation. Cinematic composition, 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

```
Molten metal pouring down heavily, sparks flying dynamically towards the camera.

## シーン8：未来へ（35-40秒）

**フェーズ:** 結び（Future）
**主人公:** 28歳（作業着）
**BGM:** フェードアウト、余韻のピアノ
**ナレーション:** （なし、または「世界の心臓を作る仕事。」）
**演出意図:** シーン1（夕焼け空を見上げる迷い）との対比 → 朝空を見上げる確信

### 開始フレーム

```
Create an anime scene in the style of Makoto Shinkai films.

Close-up of a 28-year-old Japanese male factory worker with short black hair. He is wearing dark blue work coveralls. His face is lit by bright morning sunlight from above.

He is looking upward toward the sky with determined, hopeful eyes. His expression shows confidence and resolve. Wind blows his hair slightly.

The background shows hints of factory structures, but the focus is on his face and the bright sky above. Morning atmosphere with clean, hopeful lighting. Cinematic composition, 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

### 終了フレーム

```
Create an anime scene in the style of Makoto Shinkai films.

A vast, brilliant blue morning sky fills the entire frame. White clouds drift peacefully across the expansive sky. Bright sunlight creates dramatic lens flare and god rays streaming down.

The sky is clear, open, and full of possibility. This is the sky of a new day, a new future. The atmosphere is powerful, liberating, and hopeful.

No human figures visible - just the infinite blue sky representing unlimited potential. Cinematic composition, 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

### カメラワーク指示（動画生成用）

```
Camera rapidly pans upward from the worker's face, sweeping dramatically into the bright blue sky. Dynamic, energetic upward movement with momentum. Lens flare bursts as camera reaches the sun.
```

## 生成時の注意事項

1. **キャラクターシートを先に生成** - 一貫性の基準を作る
2. **参照画像として添付** - 各シーン生成時にキャラクターシートを添付
3. **一度に1枚ずつ** - 複数同時生成は品質が下がる
4. **微調整は会話で** - 「もう少し笑顔に」など自然言語で指示
