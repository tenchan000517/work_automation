# 演出プロンプト 確定版（新海誠スタイル）

テスト済みで使用可能な演出プロンプト集。

---

## A. そのまま使える（6種）

---

### A-1. 扉開放・迷いから確信

**評価**: このまま使える！

**動画プロンプト:**
```
Makoto Shinkai style, over-shoulder view,
0-1.3s: character in dark space, searching, anxious expression,
1.3-1.9s: door outline glowing ahead, character approaches,
1.9-2.7s: hands pushing door, door slowly opening, light pouring through,
2.7-3.7s: door fully open, light and wind flooding in,
hair and clothes blown back, character silhouette in doorway,
3.7-4.9s: stepping through, new bright world visible,
beautiful Shinkai sky beyond door,
4.9-5.5s: door fading behind, character in new world,
smooth motion, 5.5-second clip, high quality, no text, no watermark
```

---

### A-2. 光の粒子上昇（Light Particles Rising）

**評価**: 意図通り、ファンタジー向け

**動画プロンプト:**
```
Makoto Shinkai style golden particles on black background,
light particles animation,
0-1s: particles dormant at bottom, dim glow,
1-1.5s: sudden ignition, brightness increases, rapid upward surge,
1.5-3s: continuous stream rising, varying speeds creating depth,
3-5s: gentle float, soft fade at top, peaceful resolution,
warm golden bokeh effect,
centered composition for character overlay,
5-second clip, high quality, no text, no watermark
```

---

### A-3. グリッチ（Digital Glitch）

**評価**: 割といい、ファンタジー感あるが使える

**動画プロンプト:**
```
Makoto Shinkai style colors with digital glitch on black background,
glitch effect animation,
0-1s: subtle scan lines, minimal interference,
1-1.5s: intense glitch burst, RGB split, chromatic aberration,
1.5-3s: sustained glitch variation, color channel separation,
3-5s: decreasing frequency, return to stability,
horizontal displacement, block artifacts,
cyberpunk aesthetic with Shinkai color palette,
5-second clip, high quality, no text, no watermark
```

---

### A-4. エネルギー波動（Energy Aura）

```
Makoto Shinkai style energy on black background,
energy aura wave animation,
0-1s: dormant energy at center, faint flickering,
1-1.5s: energy ignites, bright flash, first wave expands,
1.5-3s: continuous pulsing waves, blue purple cyan palette,
3-5s: waves establish rhythm, intensity decreases, breathing oscillation,
auroral ribbon effects, soft gradient falloff,
centered composition for character overlay,
5-second clip, high quality, no text, no watermark
```

---

### A-5. 色彩浸食・新しい価値観の浸透

**評価**: 再現度高め、アリ

**動画プロンプト:**
```
Makoto Shinkai style color palette,
0-1s: character in monochrome or blue-gray, flat expression,
1-1.5s: touching something (hand, light), contact point glows,
1.5-3.5s: color spreading from contact like ink in water,
flowing through arm to shoulder to whole body,
expression changing from surprise to interest to joy,
background also beginning to colorize,
3.5-4.5s: full color achieved, face and eyes last to change,
4.5-5s: complete transformation, vibrant Shinkai palette,
environment fully colored, peaceful resolution,
smooth motion, 5-second clip, high quality, no text, no watermark
```

## B. 基本パターン（4種）

ミス起こりにくい安定した演出。インパクトは薄いが汎用的。

---

### B-1. 覚醒の目開き（The Awakening）

**評価**: 超アップでシンプル、ミス起こりにくい

**動画プロンプト:**
```
Makoto Shinkai style, beautiful detailed sky reflected in eyes,
extreme close-up of face, eyes slowly opening over 2 seconds,
pupils dilating, light reflecting in eyes creating sparkle,
expression changing from peaceful to aware,
soft morning light, lens flare in pupil,
subtle camera push in,
smooth motion, 4-second clip, high quality, no text, no watermark
```

---

### B-2. 運命の振り向き（The Turn）

**評価**: 安定、ミス起こりにくい

**動画プロンプト:**
```
Makoto Shinkai style, dramatic sunset sky background,
character turning around slowly, back view to front view,
long hair flowing with the movement, catching golden light,
wind blowing gently, clothes swaying,
backlit silhouette transitioning to illuminated face,
lens flare appearing as face turns toward light,
camera static, character movement creates tension,
smooth motion, 4-second clip, high quality, no text, no watermark
```

---

### B-3. 希望への手（Reaching Out）

**評価**: 手だけなのでミス起こりにくい、安定

**動画プロンプト:**
```
Makoto Shinkai style, beautiful cloudy sky with god rays,
hand reaching upward toward the light,
fingers slowly spreading open,
low angle shot capturing both hand and face,
silhouette effect with rim lighting,
dramatic clouds moving slowly in background,
camera slight tilt up following the hand,
smooth motion, 4-second clip, high quality, no text, no watermark
```

---

### B-4. 空を見上げる（Looking Up）

**評価**: ミス起こりづらい、安定

**動画プロンプト:**
```
Makoto Shinkai style, stunning detailed sky with clouds,
character with head down, looking at ground,
slowly raising face upward,
expression changing from despair to hope,
light from above gradually illuminating face,
camera following face movement, tilting up together,
beautiful sky revealed as face rises,
smooth motion, 4-second clip, high quality, no text, no watermark
```

## 使用ガイド

| カテゴリ | 用途 |
|---------|------|
| A. そのまま使える | メイン演出として即採用可能 |
| B. 基本パターン | 繋ぎ、導入、汎用シーンに |

### 組み合わせ例

- B-1（目開き）→ A-2（光粒子）合成 = 覚醒シーン
- B-2（振り向き）→ A-4（エネルギー波動）合成 = 決意シーン
- A-5（色彩浸食）→ A-6（地平線）= 変革から旅立ちへ
