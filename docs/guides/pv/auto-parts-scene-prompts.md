```
Create an anime character reference sheet in the style of Makoto Shinkai films.

The character is a 20-year-old Japanese female university student with short black hair in a cute bob cut. She has bright, curious dark brown eyes and a slim figure.

She is wearing casual clothes - a light cardigan over a simple blouse, with comfortable jeans. Her style is neat but relaxed, typical of a university student.

Show the character from multiple angles: front view, side profile, and three-quarter view. Include both full body poses and upper body close-ups.

Use a clean white background. The art style should have detailed shading, soft lighting, and the atmospheric quality seen in films like "Your Name" or "Weathering with You".
```

## シーン1：日常と迷い（0-5秒）

**フェーズ:** 導入（Past）
**主人公:** 20歳（私服・ショートヘア）
**BGM:** 静かなピアノソロ
**ナレーション:** 「私は何かを探していた。」

### 開始フレーム

```
Create an anime scene in the style of Makoto Shinkai films.

A 20-year-old Japanese female university student with short black hair sits by a cafe window at sunset. She is wearing casual clothes - a light cardigan over a blouse. She gazes out the window with a thoughtful, slightly uncertain expression.

Golden hour sunlight streams through the window, creating beautiful lens flares and warm highlights on her face. A coffee cup sits on the table in front of her. The cafe interior is softly blurred.

Her expression suggests she is searching for something in life, contemplating her future. Melancholic yet beautiful atmosphere. Cinematic composition, 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

### 終了フレーム

```
Create an anime scene in the style of Makoto Shinkai films.

Same cafe scene. The 20-year-old woman with short black hair is still by the window, but now the camera shows more of the outside view through the glass.

The sunset sky outside is dramatic with orange and purple clouds. Her reflection is faintly visible in the window glass, superimposed over the sky. She looks small against the vast sky, suggesting endless possibilities.

Poetic, contemplative mood. The boundary between inside and outside, present and future. Cinematic composition, 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

### カメラワーク指示

```
Static shot, subtle focus shift from her face to the window and sky outside. Warm light gradually intensifies.
```
